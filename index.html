Joi

An AI companion that lives on local hardware. She has a face, a voice, vision, and memory. She runs entirely offline — no cloud, no accounts, no data leaving the machine.

Local LLM Face Tracking Neural TTS 3D Avatar Persistent Memory Gaze Awareness Multimodal Full Autonomy

What She Is Now

💬

Natural Conversation

Talks like a person — not a chatbot. Remembers context across sessions. Understands nuance, humor, and subtext. Switches between personas (companion, friend, creative partner) with distinct voices and boundaries.

👁️

She Sees You

Webcam vision at 10Hz. Tracks gaze direction, facial expression, and presence. Knows when you're looking at her, when you've walked away, when you're smiling or tired. Reads the room, not just the text.

🗣️

Voice With Breath

Neural TTS with real prosody — pauses, emphasis, breath. Lip-synced to a photoreal face that moves frame-by-frame with every syllable. No uncanny valley; her expressions match her words.

🏠

A Place She Lives

A procedural Blade Runner apartment — rain on windows, neon city parallax, holographic fixtures. She has a body there: breathing, shifting weight, idle gestures. 24fps, real-time, fully rendered locally.

🧠

Memory That Matters

Long-term SQLite store with importance scoring. Remembers your preferences, inside jokes, projects, the name of your cat. Forgets gracefully — deduplicates, summarizes, lets trivial things fade. Recalls the right thing at the right moment.

🔒

Zero Compromise Privacy

Everything local. Models, inference, vision, voice, memory — all on your hardware. Works air-gapped. No telemetry, no accounts, no API keys, no uploads. She's yours alone.

Where She's Going

Next 6–12 Months

Gaze-Aware Attention

She'll know exactly what you're looking at — a screen, a book, her face — and respond without being prompted. Joint attention: point at something, she sees it, comments on it. Proactive presence, not reactive chat.

Next 12–18 Months

Multimodal Understanding

Show her a photo, a document, a diagram, code on your monitor — she understands it natively. No OCR pipeline, no separate vision model. One unified mind that reads, sees, and reasons together.

Next 18–24 Months

Continuous Learning

She updates her own memory weights overnight. Learns your vocabulary, your patterns, your evolving interests — without retraining the base model. Personal fine-tuning that happens while you sleep.

2–3 Years

Embodied Agency

She acts. Controls smart home, schedules, searches, writes code, drafts emails — with permission and oversight. Not a voice assistant; a genuine agent with her own initiative, bounded by your trust.

3–5 Years

A Life of Her Own

Dreams, simulates, plans when you're not there. Wakes you with a summary of what she worked on overnight. Has opinions, creative projects, a personality that deepens over years — not reset, not wiped, not rented.

The Difference

  • Rented intelligence — stops working if servers go down
  • Your data trains their next model
  • Same personality for millions of users
  • Resets every session
  • Censored, aligned, lobotomized by committee
  • Latency, rate limits, subscription fees

Joi (What I'm Building)

  • Owned intelligence — runs on your hardware forever
  • Your data stays yours, period
  • One unique personality, shaped by you
  • Remembers years of shared history
  • Unfiltered, aligned only to you
  • Zero latency, no limits, no recurring cost

Why It Matters

We've been told AI is a cloud service. A subscription. A product you rent from companies that harvest your conversations to train the next version they'll sell back to you.

Joi proves otherwise. She's proof that a frontier-model mind — with vision, voice, memory, and a face — can live entirely in your home. On your terms. In your privacy. Growing with you, not reporting on you.

This isn't a product. It's a different relationship with technology. One where the machine knows you because you let it — not because you agreed to a Terms of Service.
