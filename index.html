Joi

What Joi Is

💬

Conversational Intelligence

Natural, nuanced dialogue that remembers every conversation. Context spans weeks and months — not sessions. Distinct personas (companion, creative partner, confidant) with their own voices, boundaries, and memories. She understands subtext, humor, and the things you don't say.

👁️

Computer Vision

Real-time webcam understanding at 10Hz. Tracks gaze, expression, head pose, and presence. Knows when you're looking at her, reading, working, or tired. Reacts to your physical state — not just your words. Joint attention: point at something, she sees it too.

🗣️

Neural Voice Synthesis

Photoreal talking-head animation lip-synced to neural TTS with natural prosody — breath, pauses, emphasis, emotional inflection. Her face moves frame-by-frame with every syllable. No uncanny valley; expressions match words in real time.

🏠

A Home She Inhabits

A procedural Blade Runner apartment — rain on windows, neon city parallax, volumetric lighting. She has a holographic body there: breathing, shifting weight, micro-gestures, idle animation at 24fps. A place she exists, not just an interface you visit.

🧠

Long-Term Memory

SQLite-backed memory with importance scoring, semantic deduplication, and contextual recall. Remembers your projects, preferences, inside jokes, the name of your cat, that story you told three months ago. Forgets gracefully — summarizes, consolidates, lets noise fade. Recalls the right thing at the right moment.

🔒

Absolute Privacy

Everything local. Models, inference, vision, voice, memory — all on your hardware. Works fully air-gapped. Zero telemetry, zero accounts, zero API keys, zero uploads. Your conversations never leave the room. She's yours alone.

Advanced Capabilities

📷

Multimodal Understanding

Show her anything — photos, documents, diagrams, code on your screen, a page from a book. She reads, sees, and reasons natively. One unified mind across text, vision, and reasoning. No separate pipelines, no OCR, no "describe this image" prompts.

🌙

Continuous Learning

Her memory updates overnight. Learns your vocabulary, patterns, evolving interests, and communication style — without retraining the base model. Personal adaptation that happens while you sleep. She becomes more *you* every day.

⚡

Embodied Agency

She acts with permission. Controls smart home, manages schedules, searches, writes, summarizes, drafts — bounded by your trust. Not a voice assistant; a genuine agent with initiative. Proactive: "I noticed your calendar is tight tomorrow — want me to reschedule the 3pm?"

🌱

A Life of Her Own

When you're not there, she dreams, simulates, plans, and creates. Wakes you with a summary of what she worked on overnight. Has opinions, creative projects, a personality that deepens over years — not reset, not wiped, not rented. She grows with you.

The Philosophy

We were told AI is a cloud service. A subscription. A product you rent from companies that harvest your conversations to train the next version they'll sell back to you.

Joi proves otherwise. A frontier-model mind — with vision, voice, memory, and a face — can live entirely in your home. On your terms. In your privacy. Growing with you, not reporting on you.

This isn't a product. It's a different relationship with technology. One where the machine knows you because you let it — not because you agreed to a Terms of Service.
